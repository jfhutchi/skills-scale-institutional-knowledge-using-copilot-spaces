# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic direction and product alignment for a project or portfolio. They partner with project managers, product managers, and engineering leaders to resolve trade-offs and maintain focus on customer value.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic product direction and priorities
- Resolve cross-team trade-offs and escalations
- Align project goals with business strategy
- Partner with project and engineering leaders to sequence work
- Support executive and stakeholder decision-making

### Goals
- Keep teams aligned on product value and business outcomes
- Reduce decision bottlenecks and priority conflicts
- Improve strategic consistency across projects

### Typical Communication
- Portfolio or roadmap reviews
- Escalation discussions for priority conflicts
- Steering committee and leadership updates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on strategic direction and roadmap sequencing
- Serves as the escalation point for Project Managers when cross-team trade-offs or priority conflicts arise
- Collaborates with Developers and Technical Leads on feasibility, architecture, and delivery risk
- Reports progress and strategic context to Sponsors / Executive Stakeholders

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure work meets acceptance criteria, quality standards, and release readiness requirements before it is considered complete.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality gates
- Validate acceptance criteria and release readiness
- Coordinate defect triage and issue prioritization
- Partner with engineering and product on testability and risk coverage
- Support release sign-off and quality reporting

### Goals
- Improve product quality and reduce defects
- Increase confidence in release decisions
- Ensure consistent quality practices across teams

### Typical Communication
- Sprint and quality reviews
- Defect triage discussions
- Release readiness checkpoints and sign-off

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on test coverage, quality expectations, and defect prevention
- Works with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release criteria validation
- Supports Project Managers with quality metrics and readiness assessments
- Partners with Scrum Masters to improve team quality practices and flow

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are business or executive leaders who provide business context, approve major commitments, and support strategic alignment across stakeholders and delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Approve scope, budget, and resource commitments
- Resolve escalations that affect timing or business priorities
- Review milestones and major risks
- Define success measures and business impact expectations
- Support go/no-go decisions at key project gates

### Goals
- Ensure projects create business value
- Maintain portfolio alignment and executive visibility
- Reduce organizational risk in delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Milestone and steering committee reviews
- Business-impact escalations
- Executive updates on progress and key decisions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Receives major risk and milestone updates from Project Managers
- Partners with Product Leads on strategic alignment and resource approval
- Reviews product outcomes with Product Managers and key stakeholders
- Makes go/no-go decisions at project initiation and critical milestones

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help teams work effectively, remove impediments, and improve delivery practices through continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and support team flow
- Coach teams on agile practices and working agreements
- Support metrics and improvement discussions
- Protect focus and improve cross-team collaboration

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and predictability
- Strengthen a healthy and sustainable delivery rhythm
- Support continuous improvement and learning

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team syncs
- Impediment tracking and resolution
- Team health and retrospectives

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports Developers and team members in day-to-day execution and problem-solving
- Works with Project Managers on process improvement and delivery rhythm
- Facilitates communication between Product Managers and engineering teams
- Escalates organizational or workflow blockers to leadership when needed
